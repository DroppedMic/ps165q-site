The NYC DOE MySchools application is open for the 2026–2027 school year. Apply at [schools.nyc.gov](https://www.schools.nyc.gov/enrollment/enroll-grade-by-grade).

**Tours**
- Kindergarten tours: first Wednesday of each month, 9:30 AM
- Pre-K tours: second Friday of each month, 9:30 AM

Reserve a spot by emailing the main office or calling **(718) 263-4004**.

Questions about G&T testing? Visit our [Programs](/programs/) page or read the DOE's [G&T admissions guide](https://www.schools.nyc.gov/enrollment/enroll-grade-by-grade/gifted-and-talented).
