This weekend, twelve students represented P.S. 165Q at the annual District 25 STEM Fair held at Queens College. Three teams placed:

- **Best in Show** — *"How do songbirds choose their flyways?"* (Grade 4)
- **Second Place, Physical Sciences** — *"Bridge load efficiency with everyday materials"* (Grade 5)
- **Honorable Mention, Earth Sciences** — *"Heat islands in Flushing"* (Grade 5)

Our NEST teams worked on these projects through twelve weeks of after-school sessions, supported by family volunteers and high-school mentors from Townsend Harris.

Special thanks to the families who made transportation, materials, and the late-night poster sessions possible.
