On Friday evening, families packed the auditorium for the 2026 Spring Concert. Students from grades 3 through 5 performed a program of folk songs, jazz standards, and an original piece composed by our music teacher's after-school club.

The night closed with the entire fifth grade joining the chorus on stage — a tradition that started in 1998 and is now a yearly highlight.

> "The kids didn't just sing — they performed. You could feel the rehearsal in every measure." — Principal Davis-Nealy

Thank you to the families, volunteers, and our PTA for making the evening possible.
