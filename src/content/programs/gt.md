The G&T program at P.S. 165Q follows the NYC DOE's accelerated curriculum, with adjustments for our school's strengths in literacy and STEM. Students complete a full grade level of curriculum each year while engaging in independent research projects and enrichment seminars.

**What makes our G&T program distinct:**
- Cross-grade research clubs
- Annual G&T Symposium where students present original work to families
- Strong feeder pipeline to specialized middle schools in District 25
